Code is Poetry.

Written with Logic

My journey into software engineering began quite unexpectedly during the COVID-19 pandemic. While aimlessly exploring websites in that period of isolation, I accidentally pressed the F12 key. Suddenly, a hidden panel of raw text and symbols emerged on my screen. I had absolutely no idea what they were, what they were for, or how they worked. I was completely mesmerized to realize that these cryptic lines—known as source code—were the invisible gears actively powering the entire website.

That single serendipitous moment sparked a profound curiosity within me. The sheer amazement of how simple text could architect functional, interactive digital worlds quickly evolved into a dedicated pursuit of learning. It transformed a simple accident into the foundation of my career as a software engineer today.
