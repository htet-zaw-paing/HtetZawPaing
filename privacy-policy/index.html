Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. No Data Collection

This website is built as a static portfolio. I strictly do not use any databases to collect, store, or process your personal information. Your visit to this website is completely private and anonymous.

This website do not use any tracking cookies or third-party analytics trackers. The only data saved on your browser is your language preference (English or Myanmar) utilizing HTML5 Local Storage. This minimal data point never leaves your personal device.

3. Email Communications

When you choose to reach out via the 'Send Email' buttons or contact form, it redirects to your default email application utilizing standard 'mailto:' protocols. I do not intercept, read, or store your typed inputs on any server before they reach my inbox.

4. AI Assistant Chatbot

The AI chatbot provided by SupportFast is used purely to help navigate the website and answer simple questions. Any chat session data is managed temporarily by the chatbot provider and is not linked to any personal identifying information.
